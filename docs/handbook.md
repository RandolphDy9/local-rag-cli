# Apartment Rental Handbook

## Rule 1: Rent Payment

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Payments can be made by bank transfer or through the tenant portal. Cash is not accepted.

## Rule 2: Late Fees

A grace period runs through the 5th of the month. Rent received after the 5th incurs a $50 late fee, plus $10 for each additional day it remains unpaid.

## Rule 3: Security Deposit

Tenants pay a security deposit equal to one month's rent before moving in. The deposit is returned within 30 days of move-out, minus the cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## Rule 4: Lease Term and Renewal

The standard lease lasts 12 months. Tenants who want to renew must give written notice at least 60 days before the lease ends.

## Rule 5: Early Termination

Tenants may end the lease early with 60 days of written notice and an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

## Rule 6: Pets

Up to two cats or dogs are allowed per apartment, and each pet must weigh under 50 pounds. Tenants pay a one-time pet deposit of $300 and monthly pet rent of $25 per pet.

## Rule 7: Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 7 AM every day. Three documented noise complaints within 6 months count as a lease violation.

## Rule 8: Maintenance Requests

Submit routine maintenance requests through the tenant portal, and they will be addressed within 3 business days. For emergencies such as flooding or a gas smell, call the 24-hour maintenance line for a response within 2 hours.

## Rule 9: Guests

Guests may stay for up to 14 days in any 6-month period. Anyone staying longer must apply to be added to the lease.

## Rule 10: Parking

Each apartment includes one assigned parking space. A second space costs $75 per month, if available. Vehicles parked in fire lanes or without a valid permit will be towed at the owner's expense.
